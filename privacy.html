Binge for X – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Binge for X ("the extension") is a Chrome extension that shows videos from x.com in a full-screen, scrollable feed. It is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by X Corp. or X.

Short version

The developer does not collect, store, sell, or share your personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking. Everything the extension does with your X data happens inside your own browser.

What the extension reads

  • Video links and post details on x.com. To play videos in its own player, the extension reads X's responses in the page you are already viewing: video file links, like/repost/reply counts, and whether you have liked or reposted a post. This data stays in your browser and is discarded when you close the tab.
  • Your X session. To like, repost, load comments, or post a reply from inside the extension, it sends requests to x.com using your existing logged-in session (including the page's security token). These requests go only to x.com, only when you press the matching button, and are the same kind of request the X website makes. Your X credentials are never read for any other purpose, never stored by the extension, and never sent to the developer.

What the extension stores

  • Your settings (auto-advance, start muted, preload amount, video quality, progress line, auto-hide controls, Pro status) are saved with Chrome's storage. If Chrome sync is on, Chrome may sync them across your devices through your Google account. The developer cannot see them.
  • A cached sponsor message is stored locally in your browser so it does not need to be downloaded every time.

Sponsor message

The extension may download a small text file (a sponsor name, short text, and link) from a server operated by the developer. When it does, that server can see your IP address, as any website would. No other information is sent. The sponsor card is always labelled "Sponsored" and links are opened only if you click them. Nothing is sent to sponsors by the extension.

Downloads

The download button saves a video from X directly to your computer. Only download content you have the right to save.

Third-party links

The "Buy me a coffee" button opens Ko-fi, and sponsor links open other websites. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and the extension does not send them any data.

Permissions

  • Access to x.com and twitter.com: needed to add the video feed to the site and to find the videos.
  • Storage: needed to save your settings and the cached sponsor message.

Remote code

The extension does not download or run remote code. It only downloads plain text data for the sponsor card.

Children

The extension is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with an updated date.
